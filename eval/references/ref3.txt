DataBridge, an enterprise data integration platform, raised a $200 million Series C at a $1.4 billion valuation to fund international expansion into Germany, Japan, and Australia. The company reported $68 million in annualised recurring revenue, up 187% year-over-year, with net revenue retention of 138% — indicating strong and growing enterprise adoption. DataBridge targets profitability by Q2 2026 and differentiates through high customer retention in an increasingly competitive data infrastructure market.
