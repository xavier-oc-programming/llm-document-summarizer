This study demonstrates that incorporating behavioural engagement features — session frequency, feature adoption, and support ticket sentiment — significantly improves customer churn prediction in B2B SaaS. An XGBoost model trained on behavioural features achieved AUC-ROC of 0.891, compared to 0.714 for a demographic-only baseline. Session frequency in the 30 days before renewal was the strongest predictive signal, and the deployed model is estimated to prevent $2.3 million in annual churn.
