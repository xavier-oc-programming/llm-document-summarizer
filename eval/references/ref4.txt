CloudVault is updating its Terms of Service effective 1 July 2026, with key changes including a reduction in free-tier data retention from 90 to 30 days for inactive accounts, introduction of third-party analytics metadata sharing with an opt-out option, and halving of free-tier API rate limits from 1,000 to 500 requests per day. All disputes will now be subject to binding arbitration in California. Users who do not accept the new terms must delete their accounts before the effective date.
